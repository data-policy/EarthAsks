Reading the maps. The price-to-income ratio is a multiple of annual household income (×), not a percentage. Rental Affordability (%) measures households above the 30% rent-to-income threshold. Higher values mean greater affordability pressure on both maps. Each legend uses its own thresholds, adapted from Figures 3.2 and 3.4 in the report.
